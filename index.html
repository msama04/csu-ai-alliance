About Us

Students. Ideas. Real Impact.

The CSU Artificial Intelligence Alliance is a student organization at California State University, Chico dedicated to helping students explore the opportunities and challenges of artificial intelligence.

We offer workshops, hands-on projects, discussions, and networking opportunities. No prior AI experience is required — just curiosity and a desire to learn.

Who can join?

Any currently enrolled Chico State student interested in AI.

  • Free membership
  • Open to all majors
  • No prior experience required

View a short constitution summary

The Artificial Intelligence Alliance brings together CSU Chico students interested in AI for learning, collaboration, projects, discussions, and networking. Membership is free. The organization is led by a President and Treasurer, meets at least bi-monthly, and follows Chico State and CSU student organization requirements.
